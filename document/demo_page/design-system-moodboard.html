UX/UI design system — internal reference

The design system behind RFS Report Finder.

Every token on this page is copied verbatim from app/globals.css — shadcn's new-york / neutral base plus the semantic layer added in Phase 15 (document/design-system.md). Nothing here is invented; if a value looks wrong, the app's globals.css is out of date with this file, not the other way around.

Spacing & radius

Tailwind's default 4px scale, unmodified — no custom spacing in tailwind.config.ts.

Container: 2rem (32px) padding, centered, max-width 1400px at the 2xl breakpoint (tailwind.config.ts → container). Everything else follows Tailwind's stock space-1…space-96 scale.

Elevation

Cards rest at shadow-sm, raise to shadow-md on hover — nothing floats higher than that today.

Flattable rows, list items
shadow-smresting card
shadow-mdhover-raised card
Tailwind's default shadow color is plain black at low opacity in both themes — it barely reads on a near-black dark card. In dark mode, border is doing most of the work of separating a card from the page, not elevation. Worth knowing before assuming a "missing shadow" in dark mode is a bug.

Iconography

lucide-react throughout — 1.75px stroke, no fill. Badge icons stay h-2.5 to h-4; everything else is h-4 (16px) or h-5 (20px) for sidebar nav.
